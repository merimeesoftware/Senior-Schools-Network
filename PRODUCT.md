## Product purpose

`(locked in substance; wording tightened here)`

The Senior Schools Network is a foundational hub to **discover, promote, and loosely affiliate** schools and programs that form souls through **sense, story, and liturgy** — poetic knowledge, natural exploration, liturgical rhythm, and narrative immersion — rooted in Catholic and Western tradition.

It draws inspiration especially from Dr. John Senior’s *The Restoration of Innocence: An Idea of a School* and the Integrated Humanities Program (IHP) with Dennis Quinn and Frank Nelick. It is **not** a second IHP, not a franchise curriculum, and not a method kit.

**Objectives**

1. **School network promotion** — Find and highlight schools already operating in this spirit.
2. **Encouragement** — Help parents, founders, and educators connect; offer informal guidance; favor dedicated educators over standardized curricula.
3. **Digital platform** — Showcase the philosophy, share primary texts and resource lists, and connect stakeholders at [seniorschools.org](https://seniorschools.org).

## Core philosophy

### Formative chain `(locked)`

**Sense → story → liturgy → identity, purpose, meaning.**

Short public form: education through **sense, story, and liturgy** — unto **identity, purpose, and meaning**.

1. **Sense** — Earth grasped with earthly touch; exterior senses sharpened in naked contact; interior senses and emotions awakened without being exploited.
2. **Story** — Muses teach through rhyme, fable, adventure, and song. Memory thinks in stories. Mother Goose before paradigms; adventure before social-studies units.
3. **Liturgy** — Prayer, sacraments, feasts, and seasonal rhythm gather sense and story into ordered praise. Architecture of the day, not assembly filler.
4. **Identity** — The child becomes someone who has heard, seen, sung, and knelt — family, parish, civilization — not a consumer profile. *Pudor* arrives; the first look teaches who the beloved and the self might be.
5. **Purpose** — Quest and vow (romantic), then justice and governing (virtuous): keeping faith with what was seen.
6. **Meaning** — Fruit of the whole tree: wisdom that recognizes the Maker in the made. Not a week-one worksheet objective.

### Poetic knowledge `(locked as product claim; attribute carefully)`

Right order is delight and presence before analysis; stargazing and poetry before equations; trust and connaturality before laboratory certainty. Poetry is knowledge — sense, emotion, and will aligned so rhetoric, dialectic, and science have a base. Without that base, higher faculties collapse into heartless technique.

Poetic knowledge is **fertile soil** for later arts and sciences, not an anti-intellectual substitute. The network wants persons who can eventually reason — not technicians who never learned to see.

### Catholic fidelity `(locked)`

Exclusionary in doctrine, charitable in tone. Resistant to modern systematization. Hospitable to persons. Family and Church guard the enclosed garden; the State neither abolishes nor absorbs paternal authority (theme received via network axioms / *Rerum Novarum* presence in the corpus).

### Against method; educators over curricula `(locked)`

Affiliation asks: Are there real educators? Contact with things, stories, and liturgy? Wonder allowed to ripen into wisdom?  
It does **not** ask: Have you implemented our pacing guide?

Good educators beat perfect methods. You cannot give what you do not have. The network promotes persons and places, not enforced schemas.

### Tree, not rocket `(locked)`

Modes are cumulative and overlapping. Advance retains earlier modes as living roots. Ages are approximate. Restoration is the *work*; the five modes are the *sequence*.

### Diagnosis (what the guide names) `(locked in substance)`

- **Screens** invade the musical garden and evacuate the gymnasium.
- **Softness** bubble-wraps children out of arranged adventure and real contact.
- **Specialization / fragmentation** forces analysis before wonder; STEM-first pipelines poison the well; poetic and romantic collapse into premature sexualization.

### Vocabulary rules `(locked)`

- **Musical** = Muses (song, Mother Goose, delight), not band class.
- **Romantic** = medieval romance (quest), not the nineteenth-century novel of feeling.
- **Wonder** = fruit of the poetic (delight → wonder → wisdom). Never the name of mode 1.
- **Garden** and **gymnasium** = place-images under musical and gymnastic — not public mode names / filter slugs.
- **Gymno-poetic** = inner technical compound only. Refuse on buttons, filters, and first-paragraph copy.

Early lad yearning is still poetic knowledge: the beloved as image and presence. Romance is what that look must become when it rides out. Modern sexualization collapses the two; the network names them apart so the first field is not burned before the second can grow.

## Taxonomy distinction (network modes vs Senior’s modes of knowledge)

Two five-item lists live near each other. Mixing them is the most common philosophical error on the site.

### A. Network developmental modes `(locked — this PRODUCT)`

musical · gymnastic · poetic · romantic · virtuous  
→ directory filters, public cadence, resource tags.

### B. Senior’s modes of knowledge `(prefer printed Restoration list)`

In *The Restoration of Innocence*, Part II, “The Five Modes of Knowledge,” Senior lists modes on the scale of knowledge as:

1. **Gymnastic** — immediate sense-contact (*gymnos*, “naked”)
2. **Music** — knowledge by emotion / sympathy
3. **The Liberal Arts** — instrumental habits of abstracting and syllogizing
4. **Science** — intellectual knowledge by analysis and synthesis
5. **Practical Science** — application of models to particulars

He couples these, in the art of teaching, to kinds of school (gymnasium / high school / college / graduate / workplace residency). Elsewhere, under “Poetic Theme,” he also distinguishes **four thematic grades**: poetic, rhetorical, dialectical, scientific — insisting that poetry is knowledge.

### C. How the network relates them without collapsing them `(locked policy)`

- Network modes **borrow names and soil** from Senior (musical / gymnastic / poetic) and then carry the soul into **romantic** and **virtuous** as *life-provinces* of formation — not as replacements for liberal arts / science or for dialectic / rhetoric.
- Do **not** add dialectic or rhetoric as network filter stages.
- Do **not** silently rewrite Senior’s five modes of knowledge to match marketing.

### Shorthand conflict `(locked)`

Older notes glossed Senior’s modes of knowledge as “gymnastic, musical, poetic, dialectic, rhetoric.” That shorthand **does not match** the five-item list printed in *Restoration* II.1, and partially overlaps the thematic grades.

**Standing rule:** when citing Senior’s own taxonomy, use the printed *Restoration of Innocence* II.1 list (gymnastic, music, liberal arts, science, practical science). Keep the network five untouched. The shorthand “gymnastic, musical, poetic, dialectic, rhetoric” stays retired.

## Voice & quotation policy

### Voice `(proposed product voice; align with StoryBrand “meat-and-potatoes + wonder”)`

- **Clarity is the doorway; wonder is the room.** Plain hook first; poetic reward second.
- Visitor is “you.” Network is “we” only when stating the work.
